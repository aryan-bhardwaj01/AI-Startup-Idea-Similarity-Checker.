CraftLink is a platform that connects retired master craftsmen, such as carpenters, potters, weavers and metal workers, with people who want old household items repaired instead of thrown away. Every year families discard furniture, utensils and clothing that could be fixed, while skilled older artisans lose income and have no way to reach customers online. A customer uploads photos of a broken chair, a cracked clay pot or a torn handloom shawl, and the app suggests nearby craftsmen along with a fair estimated price and time. The craftsman accepts the job, collects the item or visits the home, and payment is released only after the customer confirms the repair. The platform also teaches younger people through short video lessons recorded by the artisans, so traditional repair skills are not lost, and it reports how much waste each repair has prevented.
