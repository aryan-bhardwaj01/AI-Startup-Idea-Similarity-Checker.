SkillBridge is an online platform that prepares final year engineering students in tier two and tier three colleges for their first job. Most of these students study outdated syllabi and have never built a real project, so they struggle in technical interviews even though they are capable. The platform gives each student a personalised learning path based on a short skills test, followed by small guided projects that use real tools such as Git, databases and cloud services. Working engineers review each project and give written feedback. Students also practise mock interviews with an AI interviewer that asks follow up questions and scores their answers on clarity and depth. Every completed project and score is added to a verified portfolio that students can share with recruiters. Colleges can see overall progress and placement readiness for each batch.
