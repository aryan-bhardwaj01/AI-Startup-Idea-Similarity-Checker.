MediBuddy is a mobile app and WhatsApp service that helps elderly patients in small towns and villages manage their daily medicines. Many older people take five or more tablets a day, forget doses, mix up similar looking strips, and run out of stock without telling their families. The patient or a relative photographs the doctor's prescription, and the app reads it and builds a simple schedule with voice reminders in Hindi and other local languages. It tracks refills and sends an alert to the family and the local pharmacy before medicines finish, so home delivery can be arranged. Family members get a weekly report showing which doses were taken or missed. If several doses are missed in a row, the app calls the patient and, if needed, the nearest community health worker. The service is aimed at elderly patients, their caregivers, and small pharmacies.
